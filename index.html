I am a graduate researcher in Computer Science at Binghamton University, working in the Autonomous Intelligent Robotics (AIR) Lab under the supervision of Prof. Shiqi Zhang. My research focuses on robot learning for humanoid locomotion and manipulation.

I build systems that connect human demonstrations with learned robot behavior. My work spans visual teleoperation, demonstration data, and imitation learning, with the long-term goal of general-purpose humanoid loco-manipulation.

Before graduate research, I spent eight years building production software and large-scale data platforms, including four years at PayPal. I bring software engineering, distributed systems, and production reliability experience to robotics research.
