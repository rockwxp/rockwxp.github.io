GitHub / LinkedIn

I am a graduate researcher in Computer Science at Binghamton University, working in the Autonomous Intelligent Robotics (AIR) Lab under the supervision of Prof. Shiqi Zhang. My research focuses on embodied AI and humanoid robotics.

My current research focuses on vision-based whole-body teleoperation, embodied data collection, and imitation learning for humanoid robots. I develop policies based on Action-Chunking Transformers (ACT) that learn from human demonstrations and evaluate their closed-loop behavior in simulation. My broader interests include Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, sim-to-real transfer, and autonomous humanoid navigation.

Before graduate research, I spent eight years building production software and large-scale data platforms, including four years at PayPal. I bring software engineering, distributed systems, and production reliability experience to robotics research.
